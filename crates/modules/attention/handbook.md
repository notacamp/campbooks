## What needs you

Home lists what needs the member. Each entry is a need. A need is about one conversation.

### Needs

- A conversation has at most one open need.
- A need is raised from Scout's answers about the newest inbound message, from a rule the member taught, or by the member.
- A need comes from a message sent within the last 14 days.
- Messages sent from the inbox itself never raise a need.
- Mail in Spam or Trash never raises one.
- A new inbound message works the need out again from that message.

### The reasons

- Reply: someone is waiting for the member's answer.
- Do: the email asks the member to do something.
- Pay: money may be owed. Campbooks cannot see the bank, so this is a question: is it paid? The member can say Already paid or Still to pay.
- Review: something to check. It is a warning for suspicious mail, or a question, such as "Still needed?".
- Nudge: the member is waiting for someone who has not written back, and it is time to follow up.
- Worth a look: mail from a sender the member asked to see ("Highlight them"). Nothing is asked of the member. It moves nothing.
- Waiting: the next move is someone else's. It is not in the list. It is shown apart.

A need is either sure or a check. A check is a question for the member. A message Scout is unsure about may raise a Review question instead of a sure need. Suspicious mail raises Review, never any other need.

### Order of the list

Home orders needs by priority, whatever their reason. Each need is in one band, by the first rule it meets:

- Now: a warning about suspicious mail, or anything past its due date.
- Today: due today or tomorrow, a task planned for today, a nudge, or a person who has waited 3 days or more for a reply.
- This week: due in the next 7 days, or a person waiting for a reply or a choice.
- Whenever: no date, from services, or kept on Home by the member's rule (Worth a look).

Inside a band: warnings first, then dated before undated and the soonest date first, then people before services, then the oldest first. Each need says why it is in its band, such as "Overdue · 2 days" or "Marta has waited 4 days". Waiting is not in the list. Waiting is never counted in what needs the member.

### Invitations on Home

An invitation the member has not answered is a Decide need, from the member's provider calendars that are on. Its date is the day of the event: "Event today", "Event tomorrow" or the day. It is never overdue: it leaves Home once the event starts, or once the member accepts, declines or says maybe, or the event is cancelled. A calendar that is off shows none. An event the member organised never asks.

### Tasks on Home

Home shows a task only when it is for today: overdue, due today or tomorrow, planned for today, or back from Later. The member's other tasks are in Tasks, and Home says how many.

### Due dates and overdue

- Scout reads a due date from the email's words, relative to the day it was sent. If the words do not resolve, there is no due date.
- A need that is past its due date is overdue.
- Urgent means Scout read that the message is urgent today.
- A need long overdue, more than 7 days past its due date, leaves the list. Pay and Nudge do not leave. A bill or a nudge stays until the member answers it.

### How a need leaves the list

- Replied: the member sent a message after the one that raised it. Clears Reply and Do.
- Archived: the conversation is archived. Clears Reply and Review.
- Trashed: every message is in Trash or Spam, or removed.
- Done: the member says it is done.
- Not needed: the member says it was not needed. Scout learns from it, except for a still-needed question.
- Superseded: a newer message changed the need, or reading the message again raises none.
- A rule: a rule with surface never.
- Opened: the member opened the message of a Worth a look need.
- Lapsed: the ask stopped being possible.
- Heard back: the person a Waiting or Nudge need waits on has written.

Done and Not needed can be undone. Pay also has Already paid.

### Asks that lapse

Some emails ask for something that stops being possible, such as a sign-in link or a code that expires, or an offer that closes. Scout reads that time. When it passes, the need is cleared as lapsed, without the member doing anything. Pay needs, Worth a look needs and phishing warnings do not lapse.

### Old asks: "Still needed?"

A sure Do or Reply need whose message is older than 14 days, with no due date and no lapse time, becomes a Review question: "Still to do?" or "Still needs a reply?". Yes makes it the member's own need again, and it is not asked again. No clears it as not needed.

### The clock of needs

Some needs change with time alone: a lapsing ask, an old ask turning into a question, a Waiting need turning into a Nudge. Campbooks keeps the time each will change and works them out every minute. No member action is needed.

### Later

A member can put a need off with Later (snooze), until a time they pick, up to a year ahead. Later is private to that member. Other members still see the need. It ends early when the sender writes in that conversation again.

### Who sees a need

A member sees only needs in inboxes they belong to. In an inbox they can only read, the member can only use Later.
