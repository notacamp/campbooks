# Proposal

## Why

Home's "Scout noticed" (#169) showed one guess at the bottom of a long list
and offered one answer: a rule the member might not want. A member who likes
a sender's mail (a newsletter they read, but never open in time) was told
"you archived 88 of them unread, archive them as they arrive?" and could only
say yes to that, no, or never. There was no way to say "I like these".

- The note sits below every group, so it is met last, or never.
- The goal is Scout's guess, take it or leave it.
- There is nothing between "pin it" (`surface: always`) and "hide it".

Tracking issue: https://github.com/notacamp/campbooks-cloud/issues/274

## What Changes

- **Higher on Home** (`attention` client): "Scout noticed" renders right after
  the first group (and where the groups would be when nothing needs the
  member), at 375px and on desktop. Not while Home loads.
- **A goal that can be corrected** (`conversations` client): the note's buttons
  are the notice's own verb ("Archive them…", "File under Updates…"),
  "Something else…" (with the visor), "Not now" and "Don't suggest this", in
  one style. The first two open the smart-rule confirmation, which now starts
  with "What should happen to <sender>'s mail?" as choice cards: Scout's guess,
  Highlight them, File them under Newsletters, Mark them read, and "Or tell
  Scout in your words" (read through `assistant.compile_instruction`, the path
  Scout's memory teaches by). The sentence, the preview and "apply to the mail
  already here" follow the goal; saving any goal answers the notice. `ChoiceCards`
  gains a `tag`.
- **A new rule action, `worth_a_look`** (`campbooks-ai`, `conversations`): "Highlight
  them". A rule with it keeps each new matching message in front of the member
  until they have opened it once. It hides nothing and is not `surface: always`.
- **A new need and Home group, "Worth a look"** (`conversations`, `attention`,
  `lib/ui`): reason and question `worth_a_look`, shown last after Pay with a
  hue of its own (`signal-look`). The need clears itself as `opened` once the
  message is read (here or at the provider), and offers Seen (Done) and Snooze.
  `conversations.mark_read` / `mark_unread` now work the need out again.
- Migrations widen the `reason`, `question` and `cleared_because` checks (and
  the attention `reason` check), `NOT VALID` then validated.
- OpenAPI, the `need_changed` contract and the client types are regenerated by
  hand to match (CI checks them).

## Non-goals

- No change to what Scout notices or to its numbers (`rule_notices.analysis`).
- No pinning: `surface: always` stays its own rule ("never hide").
- "Highlight them" is not offered by the instruction compiler (`assistant`):
  words that mean it map to what rules the compiler knows; the card is the way.
- No new endpoint: the free-text goal uses the existing compile operation.
- No change to how a provider's read state is synced (it already is).

## Impact

- Modules: `conversations`, `attention`, `campbooks-ai` (engine); clients
  `conversations`, `attention`, `lib/ui`.
- No new module and no cross-module dependency (the client calls the compile
  operation through `lib/api`, not through the `assistant` module).
