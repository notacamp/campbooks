- **Sends and mail changes are bound to the mailbox they were asked through.** A send or a change you make (archive, read, trash and the rest) is now bound to the connection and mailbox your inbox went out through when you made it (for a scheduled send, when you scheduled it), and Zoho's "back where it was" place after the trash is the folder Zoho itself reports. If a database row is edited afterwards to point it elsewhere, nothing is sent or changed at the provider: the send fails and the change is skipped. Sends and changes waiting at the upgrade still go out, as long as their mailbox has not changed.
