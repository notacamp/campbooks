- **Sends and mail changes go out only through the mailbox you used.** A send or a change you make (archive, read, trash and the rest) is now bound to the connection and mailbox it goes out through, and Zoho's "back where it was" place after the trash is bound to the change that trashed it. If a database row is edited to point it elsewhere, nothing is sent or changed at the provider: the send fails and the change is skipped. Sends and changes waiting at the upgrade still go out, as long as their mailbox has not changed.
