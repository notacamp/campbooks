- **A trusted sender's images follow every change to the rule.** When a smart rule that
  shows a sender's images is removed or paused, whether in Scout's memory, from the
  assistant, on another device or by another member of a shared inbox, the web app drops
  the copies of that sender's mail it had kept with their images: mail open on screen
  reloads with its images held back, and a folded message or a conversation you come back
  to waits for a fresh copy before showing anything, so its images never load from what was
  kept. The desktop and mobile apps do not hear about changes made elsewhere as they happen
  yet, so there a trusted sender's mail is checked again every time you open it (shown with
  its images held back until the check answers), every minute while it stays open and when
  you come back to the app. The choice to trust one address now says exactly what is
  checked: images show for mail from that address when its domain's checks pass, and those
  checks vouch for the domain, not for one person.
