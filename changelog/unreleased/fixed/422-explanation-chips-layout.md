- **Scout's explanation stays readable when a point comes from several messages.** On a
  wide screen, a point that cited two or more messages had its text squeezed to one letter
  per line, and the message chips spilled into the next column. The chips now sit in a row
  under the point and wrap there, so the text always gets the column's full width; a chip
  too long for the column shortens with "…". A message you sent is labelled "You", as it is
  in the thread, and a sender known only by an email address is named by the part before
  the @.
