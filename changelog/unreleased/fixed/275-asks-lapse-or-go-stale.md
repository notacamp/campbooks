- **Asks that lapse or go stale leave Home or become a question.** Home kept "<sender>
  asked you to do something" for a sign-in link that expired ten minutes after it was sent,
  and Scout's summary still said "expires in 10 minutes". Now the summary Scout writes when
  a need opens also reads, from the email's own words and when it was sent, when what it
  asks stops being possible (a link or a code expiring, an offer closing) and by when you
  are asked to act. An ask whose time has passed leaves Home at once and its conversation
  shows "Expired" in the inbox; bills never lapse and stay until you answer them, even past
  their due date. An undated request or question older than two weeks that you never
  confirmed comes back under Review as "Pedro Alves asked 3 weeks ago. Still to do?" (or
  "wrote … Still needs a reply?"): Yes keeps it for good, No clears it, and neither
  teaches Campbooks anything, since time passed rather than Scout misreading the mail.
  Summaries no longer count down ("in 10 minutes", "today"): they say how long something
  was valid after it was sent. Open asks are settled on upgrade, and summaries written
  before are read once more for their dates, within the daily AI limit.
