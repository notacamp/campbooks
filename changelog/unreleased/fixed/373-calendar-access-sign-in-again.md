- **Add calendar access no longer fails silently** (Settings → Calendar, campbooks-cloud#373). When adding calendar access needs a recent sign-in, the page now says so and offers "Sign in again", which brings you back to Settings → Calendar. Other refusals (too many tries, a provider that cannot do it, anything else) each have their own short sentence. Adding calendar access now also asks the provider for the calendar permission when you reconnect.
