- **Table cells that do not fit end in an ellipsis, and the phone's "New row" has one "+".**
  A number, a date or a tag in a narrow column used to be cut with no sign
  that there was more; it now ends in an ellipsis and the whole value shows on hover. On a
  phone the add-column "+" no longer lands under the first column, so "New row" shows a
  single "+".
