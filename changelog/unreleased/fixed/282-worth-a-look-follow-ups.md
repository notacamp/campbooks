- **Worth a look only raises for senders Campbooks can trust.** "Highlight them" now puts
  mail on Home only when the receiving provider has authenticated the sender, never for mail
  that fails its sender checks, pretends to be someone else or looks suspicious, even from an
  address you highlighted.
- **Highlighting mail already here raises just the latest one.** Saving "Highlight them" with
  "apply to the mail already here" now puts only the newest unread email on Home (it says so:
  "the latest one already here"), not every unread one. New mail still raises one item each.
- **Worth a look no longer counts as "need you".** It asks nothing of you, so it stays out of
  "N things need you" and the Home badge, keeping its own count in its group. A Home with only
  Worth a look items says "Nothing needs you" and how many emails are worth a look. In a shared
  inbox the confirmation now says the item shows for everyone and clears for all once anyone
  opens it.
- **Scout noticed has keyboard shortcuts.** Shift+F (its own verb), Shift+O (Something else),
  Shift+L (Not now) and Shift+D (Don't suggest this), shown on the buttons.
