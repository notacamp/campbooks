- **Scout can wait on a shared mailbox.** When you write to `info@` or `geral@` and wait for
  an answer, that mailbox can now be the one Scout waits on and nudges. Before, only
  addresses that look like people were offered. A shared mailbox that shows as "Geral" or
  "Info" is now named for its organisation ("Nudge Aguas Ribatejo"), not for the role.
