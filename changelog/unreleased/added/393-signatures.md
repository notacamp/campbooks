- **Your signature, per mailbox.** Settings → Mailboxes has a "Your signature" editor for each mailbox you can send from, with bold, italic and links, up to 2,000 characters, saved with Save or Cmd+Enter. In a shared mailbox each member signs with their own. A new reply, forward or message starts with your signature under the text, muted, with Leave off, Add signature (to bring it back) and Edit in Settings. It goes out after your text, after a `-- ` line in the plain-text version and in its own block before the quoted history in the formatted one. Changing your signature later never changes a message you already started.
