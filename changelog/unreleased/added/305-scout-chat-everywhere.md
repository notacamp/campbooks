- **Ask Scout from anywhere, and find your chats again.** "Ask Scout" now also opens the
  chat from a Scout noticed suggestion on Home ("Why do you suggest this?"), from a rule after
  you save it in Teach Scout and from each rule's More menu, from the Reply and Nudge dialogs
  (which stay open), and from each row of Waiting on others. In the command menu (⌘K), "Ask
  Scout" starts a chat about nothing in particular, and with words typed, "Ask Scout: …" asks
  them straight away. "Recent" in the chat's header lists your chats by their first question
  and reopens one. Chats stay private to you and kept 30 days.
