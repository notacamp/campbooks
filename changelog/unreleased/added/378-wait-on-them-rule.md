- **Teach Scout once whose move it is.** When you answer "Nudge Ana…" on a mail Ana's colleague
  sent to someone else with you in Cc, and give the same answer for the same sender a second
  time, Home and the reader offer: "When Ana writes to someone else with you in Cc, should
  Scout wait on them for you?" **Yes, always** opens the smart-rule confirmation, which alone
  saves; **Ask me each time** turns the offer down. The new rule, "Mail from Ana to someone
  else with you in Cc: wait on them", makes each such mail wait on the person it was written to,
  with a nudge suggested after the usual working days. It reads addresses only, answers nothing
  when the mail went to several people, never overrides a reply you taught, and never sends
  anything on its own. Undo of the answer cancels the offer.
