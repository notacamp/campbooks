- **A Calendar place.** Scout puts the bookings, trips, appointments, charges and deliveries
  it finds in your email on your Campbooks calendar, each linked back to its email, with
  the sentence it read the date from. See your days as an agenda, a week or a month
  (keys 1, 2 and 3), open any event to change it, answer an invitation or remove it, and
  take it back with Undo for ten seconds. Settings has a Calendar section for your time
  zone, working hours and which kinds of event Scout adds. Scout can also answer "what is
  on Friday?" and "when am I free for an hour?" from your calendar. Google and Zoho
  calendars are not connected yet.
