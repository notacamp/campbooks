- **Scout turns what an email asks of you into tasks.** When Scout reads an email in your own
  inbox and finds something you have to do or pay ("send the signed lease by Friday", "pay invoice
  2026-114"), it adds it to your tasks with the date and amount it found, and Home shows that
  task instead of the email's Do or Pay line. A task opened this way says "Scout found this in the
  email". If an earlier task already covers it, nothing is added (a date is filled in only when
  the task had none), and finishing the task finishes the line on Home. Emails in a shared inbox
  still use "Make it a task".
