- **Tell your calendars from the ones you follow.** Every calendar is now Mine, Following or Off.
  A calendar someone shared with you starts as Following: you see its events, but they do not make
  you busy, no time is held on it, and its invitations stay off your Home. A calendar you own starts
  as Mine. Scout counts only your own calendars when it looks for free time. The screens to change a
  calendar's role follow.
