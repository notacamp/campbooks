- **More control while you write.** In the composer, From is a picker whenever you can send from more than one mailbox, for every kind of message: it moves the draft to that mailbox, and your signature there goes with it (a reply keeps its `In-Reply-To` and `References`, so the recipient's mail still threads it). The title switches between Reply, Reply all and Forward, keeping what you wrote. The subject shows and can be edited on every kind. On a reply, reply all or nudge, Scout (Cmd+J) can make your text shorter or warmer, fix its spelling, or change it as you ask; it works in place on plain text, shows its thinking, and Undo puts your text back.
