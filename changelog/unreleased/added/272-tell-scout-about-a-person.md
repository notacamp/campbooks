- **Tell Scout about a person.** Under the quote in the Reply dialog, "Tell Scout about Marta"
  opens one field for who they are and how to write to them ("My landlord. Formal, in
  Portuguese."). Scout's drafts to that person follow it for tone, form of address and language,
  and "Rewrite with this" shows the difference at once. Notes are yours alone, never your
  teammates', and a message that fails its sender's checks never gets them. Scout's memory lists
  them under "People you told Scout about", with Edit and Remove (with Undo).
