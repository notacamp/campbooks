- **Workspaces, members and signups are now in the record of actions.** Workspaces
  created, renamed, deleted and purged, people invited, joining, changing role, leaving
  or removed, members paused while their account is suspended, and signup requests and
  codes decided, minted, revoked and spent are tracked with who did it and ids, roles,
  states and counts: never a workspace's name, an address, a name or a code.
