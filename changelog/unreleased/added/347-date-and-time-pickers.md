- **Date and time pickers.** Every date and time Campbooks asks for (a task's due date and plan,
  an event's day and times, working hours, a sign-up code's expiry) now uses one month grid and
  one time list, in your language, the same on every device. The grid works from the keyboard
  (arrows, Page Up and Down, Enter, Escape), and a time can be typed as you would say it: "930",
  "21h15", "9pm".
