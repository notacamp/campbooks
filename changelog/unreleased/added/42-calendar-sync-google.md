- **Your Google Calendar in Campbooks, both ways** (Calendar, Settings). Add calendar access to your Google account once and your primary calendar appears next to your Campbooks calendar; turn on the others when you want them. Events you create, move, answer or delete in Campbooks reach Google, and what changes in Google comes back. If an event changed in Google before your edit arrived, Google's version is kept and the event says so. "Add to my calendar" copies something Scout found in an email to your Google calendar. Self-hosters: enable the Google Calendar API on the Google project and add the two calendar scopes to its consent screen (see the deploy guide); without it your mail keeps syncing and the calendar says why it cannot.
