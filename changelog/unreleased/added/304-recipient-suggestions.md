- **People suggested as you type.** When you write an email, To, Cc and Bcc suggest the people
  you exchange mail with from two letters, by name, address or domain: the ones you write to
  most first. Use ↑ and ↓ and press Enter or Tab to add one, or tap it; Esc closes the list.
  Someone already on the email isn't suggested again, and no-reply addresses or senders whose
  mail failed its checks or was reported aren't suggested unless you have written to them.
