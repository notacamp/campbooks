- **Scout proposes, you confirm.** In the chat, Scout can now propose a change to an email
  (archive, star, label, move to a stream, Done, Later until Monday…), a rule or a reply. Each
  is a card naming exactly what and where: Confirm sends your own request, with Undo; Not now
  leaves it. A rule card opens Teach Scout and a reply card opens the Reply dialog; Scout never
  sends or saves anything itself, and never acts because an email asks it to.
