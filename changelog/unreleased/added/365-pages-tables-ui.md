- **Tables you can see and edit** (behind the same switch as the tables API). A table is a page: its views are tabs (a table and a board), the filter is a chip, and Filter, Sort and New row sit in one row. Cells edit where they are with a field or picker for every type (text, number, select, status, date, person, files, links, Campbooks links, relations), with Enter to edit, Esc to cancel, arrows to move, N R for a new row and ⌘↵ to open the row. Every change has an Undo, and a cell someone else changed meanwhile says so. A column is added from a type menu (Basic, Links, Filled in for you); its menu renames, retypes, sorts, filters, hides and deletes it. The board has a column per stage with its count and sum, and a card moves by drag or by its Move to menu. A row opens beside the table with its properties and its page, or as a page of its own. `/table` makes a table in a page and `/link to table` shows an existing one; a table you can't open shows as exactly that. On a phone the grid scrolls inside its box and a row opens full screen. The address holds the view and the row, so a link opens them.
