- **Scout noticed sits higher on Home, and its goal can be changed.** The note now comes
  right after the first group instead of below all of them. Its buttons are the suggestion's
  own verb ("Archive them…"), "Something else…" (with the visor), "Not now" and "Don't suggest
  this", and the first two open the smart-rule confirmation, which now starts with "What
  should happen to <sender>'s mail?": Scout's guess, **Highlight them** (new), file them under
  Newsletters, mark them read, or tell Scout in your own words. Whatever you pick answers the
  suggestion, so it never comes back.
- **"Highlight them", and a Worth a look group on Home.** A new smart-rule action keeps each
  new email from a sender in front of you until you have opened it once: it waits on Home
  under "Worth a look" (the last group, after Pay), with Seen and Snooze, and leaves by itself
  when you open it, here or on your phone. It hides nothing and pins nothing, so the mail can
  still be archived like any other. Opening a conversation (or marking it read or unread)
  now updates its Home item straight away.
