- **What you do in Campbooks reaches Outlook, and you can send from it (off by default).** Where
  the instance turns Microsoft mail on, marking a message read, starring it, archiving, deleting,
  restoring, reporting spam or moving it to a stream now happens in the Outlook.com, Hotmail, Live,
  MSN or Microsoft 365 mailbox too: "delete" moves it to Deleted Items, never deletes it for good,
  and a stream goes to your `Campbooks/<Stream>` folder where you have one, else to an Outlook
  category of that name. A change you make in Outlook meanwhile wins. Replies and new messages go
  out through Microsoft, once, threaded, filed in Sent Items by Outlook itself, from the mailbox's
  own address only and only once that address is verified; a message over Microsoft's 4 MB limit
  is not sent.
