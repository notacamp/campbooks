- **"You replied. Is this done?"** When you reply in the email thread a Do task came from, the task now asks. Home puts it first in its row ("You replied · 2 hours ago", "You answered Marta's email. Is this task done?") with **Done** (1) and **Not yet** (2), the thread on O and Ask Scout on `.`; the Tasks list marks the row "You replied · Done?", the task's detail asks above where it came from, and the thread's "Where things stand" note asks too. Not yet keeps the task open and Scout asks again only after a newer reply; both answers have an Undo. Scout never closes the task for you and does not read your reply: it only knows you wrote in the thread. Bills (Pay tasks) are never asked, since a reply does not pay them.
