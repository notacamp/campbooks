- **Send later.** The chevron beside Send offers Tomorrow morning, the next working day (Monday on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday) and Pick a time, each at 08:00 unless you choose another time, in your time zone, with the moment it means shown on each choice. The composer closes with "Scheduled: Tue 29 Oct, 08:00" and an Undo that cancels the send and brings the draft back. Scheduled sends wait in their own group at the top of Drafts, with Cancel send; opening one offers to cancel the send and edit the draft. A time that has passed or is more than a year away is turned down with the reason. Send still leaves now, after the undo window (Ctrl or Cmd Enter).
- **A scheduled send that stops now tells you.** If the mailbox changed, or you lost the right to send from it, before a scheduled send was due, nothing is sent and the draft says "Not sent" with the reason, in Drafts and in the composer. A send that is past its time stays in the Scheduled group as "Should have left …", so you can cancel it. If a scheduled send is lost altogether (after a restore, say), it is given up within a quarter of an hour: "We couldn't confirm this was sent. Check your Sent folder before sending it again." At most as many sends as the send rate allows can be scheduled for the same minute.
