- **Invitations on Home.** An invitation you have not answered is now a Decide row on Home,
  in its band by the day of the event ("Decide · Event Thu 29"), with who asked, when and
  where, and whether you are free then. Accept (1), Decline (2) or Maybe (3) right there;
  the toast's Undo takes the answer back before it reaches the organiser, since an answer
  now waits 10 seconds before it goes to your calendar provider, in Calendar too. It counts
  in "N things need you", and Scout's list of what needs you names it.
