- **Scout proposes changes to your tasks.** Tell Scout in a chat that you paid a bill, will do
  something on Friday or want Rui to take a task, and it puts a card under its answer: Mark paid,
  Plan it for Friday, Later, Due on, Hand to Rui Matos. The card names the task from your list,
  never from Scout's words, and nothing changes until you press Confirm; Undo puts it back unless
  the task changed meanwhile. Scout checks first (a task you cannot change, one already closed, a
  day in the past, a teammate who is not in your workspace) and cannot create or rename a task.
