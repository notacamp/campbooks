- **Signing in and account security are now in the record of actions.** Sign-ins that
  succeed or are refused (and why), new accounts, sessions ended one by one or everywhere,
  ways in and passkeys added, renamed and removed, suspensions, apps registered, rotated
  and removed, consents given, refused and taken back, and tokens issued, reused and
  revoked are tracked with who did it and ids, states and counts: never an address, a
  name, a token, a secret or an IP address.
