- **Pages, for what you write.** A new place (`g p`, new page `n p`) with a tree of pages, a block
  editor (type `/` for headings, lists, to-dos, toggles, callouts, code, images and files) and
  `@` to mention a person, a thread, a task, an event or another page. Several people can edit
  one page at once and nobody's words are lost. A page is private to you or open to the
  workspace, and can be shared with named members as reader, writer or manager; every page
  shows where it is linked from, deleted pages wait 30 days in the trash, and search finds pages
  by title and text. Off by default: set `CAMPBOOKS_PAGES__ENABLED=true` to turn it on.
