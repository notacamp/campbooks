- **Connect any mailbox over IMAP.** Yahoo, iCloud, Fastmail, GMX and a custom domain at a
  hosting company can be connected with an app password: Campbooks finds the mail servers
  from the address, signs in to them to check the password before keeping it, and keeps the
  password encrypted like any other credential. Gmail, Zoho and Outlook addresses are sent to
  their own sign-in instead. Off by default (`CAMPBOOKS_CONNECTORS__IMAP__ENABLED`) until
  reading these mailboxes lands.
