- **A task list for daily things.** Tasks is a new place (`g t`) for what you have to do or pay:
  type a title and pick Today, Tomorrow or a date, and press Enter. Each task can be planned for a
  day or put off until Later, handed to one teammate by name (you still see it as "With Marta" and
  can take it back), and marked Done, Paid or Not needed, always with an Undo. In an email, "Make
  it a task" (`n t`) starts one from the subject. Open tasks show in Do and Pay on Home, and a task
  made from an email replaces that email's own Do or Pay line, so one obligation shows once.
