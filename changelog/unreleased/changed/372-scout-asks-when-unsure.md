- **Scout asks when it is unsure** instead of showing a guess as sure. A reply Scout is
  only 0.8 to 0.9 sure of, or one it read on mail you were only copied on, is now a question
  ("Scout isn't sure") and no longer a "Reply" card; a guess below 0.8 shows nothing. On a
  copy of mail between others, the question offers Nudge them, Reply or Nothing for me, and
  Nudge makes the conversation wait on that person. Needs already open are worked out again
  once. Whose move it is now has its own switch,
  `CAMPBOOKS_CONVERSATIONS__MOVES__ENABLED` (off by default), apart from the action
  catalogue's.
