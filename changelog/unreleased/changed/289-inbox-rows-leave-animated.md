- **Rows say what happened as they leave the inbox list.** When you mark a conversation Done,
  report it as spam, delete it or move it to another stream, its row now shows what happened
  ("Done", "Reported as spam", "Moved to Newsletters") for a moment, then folds away while the
  rows below close the gap, so you can see what changed. Spam and Delete show in red. Undo still
  brings the row back where it was. With reduced motion turned on, the row goes without the
  animation.
