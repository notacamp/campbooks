- **Scout asks "Done?" only when a reply can do the ask.** When Scout reads an email it now
  also notes whether replying is itself how the ask gets done (an answer, an approval, a
  choice) or whether it is done another way (sign and send a form, upload, file, go somewhere).
  "Done?" after your reply is asked for the first kind only, so "I'll sign it tonight" no longer
  prompts a question. A task's detail also quotes the sender's own sentence that asked for it,
  shown only to members who can read that email.
