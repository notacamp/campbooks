- **Reply sits with Reply all and Forward, and the actions fit on one row.** On a laptop or
  desktop, Reply (R) is now at the top of an open conversation, right after Done and before
  Reply all and Forward, instead of alone at the bottom of the screen. The actions after Done
  are icons; hover one to see what it does and its key. When the window is too narrow for
  all of them, Delete, Report spam, Pin, Mark unread, Previous and Next move into a "More
  actions" (…) menu, so the actions never take two rows, on phones too. Their keys work
  either way. The bottom of the screen stays free for the thread and only holds the reply
  box once you start writing.
