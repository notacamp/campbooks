- **The email reader says what Scout found.** Under an email's header, a strip lists the
  dates Scout read in it (a flight, an appointment, a delivery), says when one is already on
  your calendar with Undo, and says "Scout is reading this" while it works. The summary of
  an email is now Scout's own read of the message, and Scout reads an email when it matters
  or when you open it, not every message.
