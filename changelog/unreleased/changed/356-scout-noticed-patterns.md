- **Scout noticed finds the kinds of mail within a sender's mail.** A shop whose promotions
  you ignore but whose shipping notices you read is now offered a rule for the promotions
  alone, named by a real subject ("40 emails like “Deals of the week 40”"), and "See the N
  emails" shows just those. A sender whose mail has no consistent kind gets no suggestion.
  Where subjects all differ, the `complete` model may group a few senders per inbox a day
  by their subjects alone, only when it may read mail on your server; the rule you save
  runs without it.
