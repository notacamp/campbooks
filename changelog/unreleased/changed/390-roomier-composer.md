- **Writing a message has more room, and formatting shows as formatting.** The text area now
  starts at about ten lines, grows with what you write, and the fields are the size of the text.
  The Send button stays in view while the text scrolls. Bold, italic, strikethrough, links with
  words, bulleted and numbered lists and quotes show as they will look, each with its key shown
  on hover; on a narrow screen the toolbar stays one row and the rest sits under More. Link asks
  for the words and an address, and only web and email addresses are accepted. On a wider screen,
  Expand (Shift+Cmd+E) opens the same draft as a sheet over the page. What is sent keeps the same
  simple formatting, and links with words and strikethrough now arrive as links and strikethrough
  in the recipient's mail.
